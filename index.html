Sustainability Reweave

Giving unloved clothes and furnishings a second life.

6ways to reweave

Reweave is a refurbished-lifestyle brand I am building around one simple idea: textiles should be reused, reshaped and remembered, not thrown away. It turns old clothes and home furnishings into new, useful and often deeply personal things, and it works in six ways.

1

Collect & Credit

Have items you no longer want? We collect them from your home and weigh them. For every 10 kg you get a ₹500 coupon to spend on our products on the website, with no minimum purchase.

2

Memory Makeovers

For clothes or furnishings that hold memories. Our specialist visits, suggests what can be made, and shows samples from a 50+ item catalogue with fixed prices. You choose; we custom-make and deliver.

3

Alter & Trendify

Clothes you love that need alterations or a fresh, trendy look, done exactly as you suggest.

4

Custom Furnishings

Peculiar or one-of-a-kind furniture that needs custom-made covers and furnishings.

5

Pet Couture

Custom-made clothes for your pets, crafted from your own clothes.

6

Wear It Forward

Donated clothes often do not fit. We visit the orphanage or old-age home, take measurements and redesign the clothes into fresh, well-fitting outfits, at heavily subsidised rates.
